Bring trust into the technology decision.

The story

Smart-home devices were growing, but clients did not yet trust the security and privacy of voice technology. Vanguard needed to assess the effort, timing, and risks of a consumer voice strategy.

The strategy had to balance market opportunity with trust, security messaging, usage data, current systems, and legacy constraints.

The strategic action

I led research, prototypes, and security experience design, grounding the work in client usage data, analytics, current systems, and legacy constraints.

I also directed smart-home concepts that helped leaders evaluate the market, client experience, and operational readiness.

Impact beyond the roadmap

  • Customer Win: Privacy and security became core experience requirements.
  • Strategic Win: Vanguard gained a clearer position on the smart-home voice market.
  • Organizational Win: Digital leaders formed teams to support stronger security.
  • Execution Win: The strategy informed credentials-reset and profile-update experiences.

Strategic takeaway

I turned an abstract voice-device question into an actionable roadmap connecting market opportunity, client trust, security, systems, and prototypes.

What this work shows

Research and prototypes helped leaders weigh voice-interface opportunity against security and operational readiness.
