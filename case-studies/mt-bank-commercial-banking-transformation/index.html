Case Study

M&T Bank Commercial Banking Transformation

A service design program that mapped lending complexity and aligned stakeholders around a clearer future state.

Fragmented processes and repeated handoffs created friction and waste. Van turned research and journey maps into a service blueprint for commercial lending.

6 min read Updated 2026-05-01

The Story

M&T Bank’s commercial lending experience spanned customers, employees, channels, documents, handoffs, and backstage operations. Friction affected planning, underwriting, approval, closing, ongoing requirements, and loan modification.

The core problem was a fragmented service model that obscured the full journey and made improvements difficult to prioritize.

The Strategic Action

Van organized scattered feedback into a clear service design narrative. He guided service and UX designers, refined concepts, and shaped the story for senior stakeholders.

The future-state blueprint connected customer friction, employee workflows, operational waste, and product direction.

Impact Beyond the Journey Maps

  • Customer Win: The blueprint showed a less fragmented lending journey.
  • Organizational Win: Stakeholders could plan modernization as a coordinated program rather than isolated features.
  • Operational Win: The work addressed an estimated $3 million in annual waste and a department with 90% attrition in one year.
  • Execution Win: Product, design, service, and business teams gained a shared future-state narrative.

Strategic Takeaway

Van made the lending ecosystem visible and turned scattered feedback into a future-state story stakeholders could use.
