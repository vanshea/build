Capital One gesture patent

Executing on a research hunch created a patent

A self-directed prototype that turned navigation friction into a patented gesture-based interaction model.

Video summary: The video presents samples of the gesture-based interaction concept and its path from prototype to patent.

The story

Testing showed that customers struggled to find information across too many screens. The team needed a more direct interaction model that reduced navigation and learning.

The strategic action

I assembled a small team and led prototype development and technical revisions. The concept earned second place at a hackathon.

I then created a concept video and executive presentation. Collaboration with the legal team developed the idea into a patent.

Impact beyond the patent

  • Customer Win: The concept reduced the learning and effort required to navigate financial information.
  • Technical Win: The gesture model explored interaction across devices and contexts.
  • Organizational Win: A small team turned a usability problem into a working prototype and executive-ready story.
  • Innovation Win: The concept earned hackathon recognition and became a patent.

Strategic takeaway
